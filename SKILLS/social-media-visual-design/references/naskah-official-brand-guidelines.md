# Official Brand Identity Guidelines: naskah.fk

## 1. Brand Identity Overview
- **Brand Name / Handle**: `naskah.fk` (Instagram & Threads: `@naskah.fk`).
- **Brand Position**: Trusted Academic Companion (bukan sekadar jasa joki tugas).
- **Core Mission**: Membuat dunia riset, metodologi, dan penulisan ilmiah terasa lebih jelas, terstruktur, dan manusiawi bagi mahasiswa kesehatan/kedokteran.

## 2. Official Logo Construction & Anatomy
The official `naskah.fk` logo is a custom geometric lettermark **N** with integrated semantic symbols:
- **Right Vertical Stem**: Forms an **Open Book** icon, representing literature, journal reading, research methodology, and thesis writing.
- **Diagonal Bar**: Replaced by a **Dynamic Winding Path (Journey / Road)** in vivid teal/cerulean that flows upward from bottom-right to top-left.
- **Top-Left Apex**: Terminates in a sharp **Upward-Pointing Arrow**, symbolizing guidance, milestone progression, and academic success.
- **Palette**: Deep Slate/Navy Blue (`#071726`), Vibrant Teal Blue (`#00A896` / `#028090`), Clean Cream (`#F5F2EB`), and Pure White negative space.

### Logo Implementation Rules across Slide Backgrounds:
1. **Light / Cream Slides (Default)**:
   - Paste raw transparent mark directly onto the cream background in the bottom-left footer.
   - Pair with `naskah.fk` wordmark in dark navy.
2. **Dark / High-Contrast Slides (Navy `#071726` or Solid Orange `#E85929`)**:
   - Place the mark inside a clean, rounded cream badge/squircle (`radius: 20-30%`, background `#F5F2EB`) to maintain full contrast and prevent the dark navy book outlines from blending into dark backgrounds.
   - Text label next to badge rendered in cream/white.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Typeface**: **Poppins** Family (Google Fonts).
  - *Headlines & Hooks*: Poppins ExtraBold / Bold (All-Caps, tight leading ~1.1-1.15x).
  - *Sub-headlines & Callouts*: Poppins SemiBold / Medium (Sentence-case, leading ~1.25x).
  - *Body Copy*: Poppins Regular / Medium (30-36px on 1080x1350 canvas, leading ~1.3-1.4x).
  - *UI Pills, CTAs, and Metadata*: Poppins SemiBold / Bold (22-26px).
- **Secondary / Accent (Optional Display)**: Montserrat or Playfair Display Black (for specific classical editorial covers).

## 4. Carousel Slide Rhythm & Structure (5-Slide High-Density Format)
- **Slide 01 (Cover)**: Warm cream bg + Ultra-Bold hook + hand-drawn imperfect orange oval encircling the primary emotional keyword (e.g. `DITOLAK`, `REVISI`, `BINGUNG`) + pill CTA (`[ Swipe buat bedah ]`).
- **Slide 02 (Pattern Interrupt / Formula)**: Solid burnt orange bg (`#E85929`) + high-contrast navy headline + 3 dark pill badges forming a logic equation (`[ variabel jelas = ]` `[ uji statistik aman ]` -> `[ lebih siap sempro ]`).
- **Slide 03 (Deep Tone / Core Rule)**: Deep navy bg (`#071726`) + cream body copy + large highlighted orange question (`data kamu kategorik, ordinal, atau numerik?`).
- **Slide 04 (Callout / Examiner Question)**: Warm cream bg + rounded rectangular callout box with dark border + bold orange quoted question (e.g. *“Sudah uji validitas?”*).
- **Slide 05 (Action & Soft CTA)**: Warm cream bg + recap headline with orange marker highlight + dark save pill (`[ ↗ Save buat checklist Bab 3 ]`) + non-aggressive footer support line.
